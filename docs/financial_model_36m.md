## 3. Non-Dilutive Government & Grant Capital Strategy

Before issuing equity to institutional investors, apply for Pakistan's active deep-tech and climate grants:

1. **IGNITE (National ICT R&D Fund) — Seed Grant:**
   * **Grant Size:** **Rs. 5,000,000 – 15,000,000 (Non-dilutive)**
   * **Focus:** AI/IoT applications solving national industrial challenges.
   * **Fit:** WattWise's pre-emptive grid stabilization directly addresses Pakistan's circular energy deficit.
   * **Timeline:** Rolling cycles, 6–8 week approval.
2. **Punjab Information Technology Board (PITB) Startup Package:**
   * Free incubation office space at Arfa Karim Software Technology Park, Lahore.
   * Up to USD $10,000 in AWS Activate Cloud credits.
   * Legal structuring and IP filing support.
3. **SMEDA (Small and Medium Enterprises Development Authority):**
   * Energy efficiency adoption grants for export-oriented manufacturing units.
4. **World Bank / IFC Climate Innovation Program (Pakistan Cohort):**
   * Grants for industrial decarbonization and Scope 1 & 2 carbon accounting solutions aligned with EU CBAM export mandates.
