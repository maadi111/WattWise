## 1. Commercial Bank Green Financing & ESCO Monetization

Textile mills face high capital costs and foreign exchange constraints when funding energy efficiency retrofits (e.g., synchronous reluctance motors, IE4 drives, captive solar, capacitor banks). WattWise partners with tier-1 commercial banks to turn telemetry data into credit underwriting intelligence.

```
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|                 ESCO GREEN BANKING FINANCING ARCHITECTURE                             |
+---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------+
|  STATE BANK OF PAKISTAN (SBP)  | Refinance Scheme for Renewable Energy & EE           |
|  CONCESSIONARY REFINANCE WINDOW| Fixed markup at 6.0% p.a. (vs KIBOR + 2.5% market)   |
+--------------------------------+------------------------------------------------------+
|  PARTNER COMMERCIAL BANKS      | Meezan Bank Islamic Green Financing / HBL Energy     |
|  (Meezan & HBL Commercial Div) | Funds Capex based on WattWise Pre-Audit Assessment   |
+--------------------------------+------------------------------------------------------+
|  WATTWISE PLATFORM ROLE        | Certified ESCO Measurement & Verification (M&V)      |
|  (Independent Telemetry Trust) | Telemetry escrow validates debt service capability   |
+--------------------------------+------------------------------------------------------+
```

### 1.1 The Meezan Bank / HBL Alliance Framework
1. **Accredited Verification Partner:** WattWise obtains technical certification as an approved third-party energy auditor for bank credit appraisal committees.
2. **Automated M&V Audit Package:** Instead of expensive, slow engineering consultancy reports, WattWise delivers a standardized 30-day telemetry baseline report confirming exact kW waste, power factor losses, and payback projections within 48 hours.
3. **Loan Performance Monitoring:** The bank receives a real-time ESG & Energy Efficiency dashboard ensuring the financed plant modifications actually deliver projected energy reductions.
4. **WattWise Commercial Revenue:**
   - Pre-financing audit fee: Rs. 150,000 paid by the borrowing mill (waived if SaaS is retained).
   - Bank referral bounty: 0.75% of financed facility Capex upon successful debt disbursement.

## 3. NEPRA Demand-Side Management (DSM) & CTBCM Aggregation

Pakistan’s power sector is transitioning under the National Electricity Policy 2021 toward the **Competitive Trading Bilateral Contract Market (CTBCM)**, enabling bulk power consumers (>1 MW) to procure power directly from competitive generation companies rather than DISCO monopolies.

### 3.1 WattWise as a Virtual Power Plant (VPP) Aggregator

```
                                +-----------------------------+
                                |  NATIONAL GRID / CPPA-G     |
                                |  (Ancillary Services / DSM) |
                                +--------------+--------------+
                                               |
                                +--------------+--------------+
                                |  WATTWISE VPP AGGREGATOR    |
                                |  (25+ MW Controllable Load) |
                                +--------------+--------------+
                                               |
                +------------------------------+------------------------------+
                |                                                             |
   +------------+------------+                                   +------------+------------+
   |   MILL 1: SPINNING      |                                   |   MILL 20: WEAVING      |
   | (1.5 MW Curtailable)    |                                   | (0.8 MW Curtailable)    |
   +-------------------------+                                   +-------------------------+
```

### 3.2 Strategic DSM Revenue Horizons (Months 12–36)
1. **Automated Peak Curtailment:** As NEPRA institutes dynamic capacity charges, WattWise automatically orchestrates non-critical loads (e.g., blowroom humidification, chiller compressors, auxiliary pump sets) across 20 mills during system peak hours (17:00 – 21:00 PKT).
2. **Frequency Response & Spinning Reserves:** Aggregating fast-starting captive gas/diesel generators allows WattWise to offer fast spinning reserve capacity to the National Transmission & Despatch Company (NTDC) at premium capacity tariff payments.
3. **Regulatory Positioning:** By presenting aggregated load profiles directly to NEPRA, WattWise establishes itself as the primary industrial energy intelligence and DSM aggregation platform in Pakistan.
