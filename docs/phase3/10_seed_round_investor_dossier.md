## 4. Due Diligence Q&A (The Tough Questions)

### Q1: Why can't Schneider Electric or Siemens crush you?
> **Answer:** Schneider and Siemens sell multi-million rupee proprietary SCADA hardware designed for Western grid stability. They require weeks of plant shutdown, invasive transformer outages, and expensive proprietary software licenses that take 18 months to approve.  
> WattWise is a non-invasive, 3-hour clamp-on software-first system built specifically for Pakistani grid realities (frequent feeder trips, load-shedding, unannounced peak hours, fluctuating power factors, and dual-tariff generation economics). We don't sell hardware; we deliver a verified cash return on day one.

### Q2: Isn't deploying hardware in Pakistan risky due to rupee devaluation and import barriers?
> **Answer:** WattWise hardware is designed around commodity open-source microelectronics. Our core gateway is built on the ESP32 dual-core microprocessor (Rs. 1,200), off-the-shelf industrial Modbus transceivers, and certified Rogowski current sensors sourced locally or assembled in Karachi. Total hardware BOM is **<Rs. 32,000 per substation**, recouped within the first 45 days of customer SaaS subscription. We are 10x cheaper to manufacture than imported European energy loggers.

### Q3: How do you collect payments when Pakistani enterprise B2B collection cycles are notoriously slow?
> **Answer:** In Pakistan, enterprise software is treated as an optional IT cost, but **electricity and diesel bills are critical operational expenditures**. WattWise directly attacks the power bill. Under our Performance Gain-Share model, our fee is paid out of the actual documented cash savings generated that month. If a mill saves Rs. 1,800,000 on avoided peak penalties and diesel optimization, paying WattWise Rs. 300,000 is an immediate net positive trade. Furthermore, we enforce strict Net-15 terms with automatic software lockouts if accounts become delinquent.

### Q4: What is your proprietary defensible data moat?
> **Answer:** In Pakistan, industrial energy data is completely offline and fragmented. By deploying high-frequency telemetry at 20+ major textile substations, WattWise builds the first proprietary, real-time database of industrial feeder trip profiles, voltage sag signatures, motor failure precursors, and localized tariff variances across FESCO, LESCO, and K-Electric. Our Grid Outage Prediction (GOP) model gets more accurate with every mill added to the network. An incumbent cannot replicate this without physically instrumenting hundreds of industrial feeders.

### Q5: What are the Series A milestones?
> **Answer:** At 18 months post-seed:
> - **ARR:** Rs. 210,000,000+ ($>\text{USD 750K}$)
> - **Fleet Footprint:** 60+ industrial manufacturing facilities across Punjab and Sindh
> - **Gross Margin:** $>82\%$ software margin
> - **Retention:** $>95\%$ Net Revenue Retention (NRR) with zero pilot churn
