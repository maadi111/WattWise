## 3. Role 2: Field Customer Success & Substation Operations Lead

### 3.1 Candidate Profile
- **Education:** D.A.E. or B.Sc in Electrical Engineering; registered with the Pakistan Engineering Council (PEC).
- **Substation Experience:** 3–6 years hands-on experience inside 11kV/415V substations, HT/LT panels, capacitor banks, and industrial diesel generator synchronization switchgear.
- **Technical Skills:** Precision installation of split-core CTs, Rogowski coils, Modbus RS-485 daisy-chaining, Schneider PM5000 / Janitza power meters, and 4G industrial cellular modems.

### 3.2 Core Responsibilities
- Execute 3-hour rapid, zero-shutdown edge gateway installations across mill substations.
- Perform weekly physical calibration audits of current transformers and enclosure seals.
- Serve as the Tier-1 on-call incident dispatcher for SEV-1 (telemetry drops) and SEV-2 anomalies.
- Build daily trust with mill electrical supervisors, providing hands-on training for the WattWise tablet UI.

### 3.3 Compensation & Benefits
- **Base Salary:** Rs. 130,000 – Rs. 175,000 / month.
- **Mobility Allowance:** Rs. 25,000 / month fuel and vehicle maintenance reimbursement.
- **Install Bonus:** Rs. 5,000 per certified, bug-free substation installation completed under 3.5 hours.
- **Health & Life Insurance:** Comprehensive family medical cover (Rs. 1,000,000 hospitalization).

## 4. Role 3: Senior Backend & Machine Learning Systems Engineer

### 4.1 Candidate Profile
- **Stack Proficiency:** Golang (microservices, concurrency, Modbus/MQTT), Python (FastAPI, PyTorch, LightGBM, ONNX Runtime), TimescaleDB / PostgreSQL, Redis, Docker.
- **Experience:** 3–5 years building fault-tolerant high-throughput telemetry ingestion pipelines, edge IoT integrations, or algorithmic trading/forecasting engines.
- **Domain Knowledge:** Time-series feature engineering, probabilistic time-to-peak forecasting, and distributed telemetry queue architecture.

### 4.2 Core Responsibilities
- Maintain and enhance the Golang telemetry ingestion engine handling 100,000+ data points/sec.
- Manage the nightly automated ML retraining pipeline (`apps/ml/pipeline/continuous_retrain.py`).
