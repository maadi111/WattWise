### 1. Purpose & Scope of Pilot
This Agreement establishes terms for a **30-Day Zero-Risk Shadow Energy Audit Pilot** conducted by WattWise Pakistan (Pvt.) Ltd. ("WattWise") at the Client’s manufacturing facility specified above.

The pilot operates strictly in **Passive Shadow Mode**:
* **Sensors Only:** WattWise installs non-invasive, split-core current transformer (CT) clamp sensors around main incoming switchgear cables.
* **No Physical Rewiring:** Sensor clamps snap around existing insulated cables without cutting wires or interrupting factory power.
* **Zero Automation / No ATS Connection:** Absolutely no relays, contactors, or automation wires will be connected to the Client's generator or Automatic Transfer Switch (ATS). WattWise hardware will not actuate or control any machine or switchgear.
* **Zero Downtime:** Installation requires approximately 45 minutes and does not require shutting down production or de-energizing the main busbar.
